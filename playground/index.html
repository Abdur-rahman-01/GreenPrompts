Token Compression Engine

Before your prompt ever hits an LLM, GreenPrompt strips redundancy, filler words, and inflated phrasing — shrinking prompts by up to 40%. Fewer tokens = less compute, less energy, less CO₂ emitted per query.

3-Tier Intelligent Routing

Every prompt is scored for complexity and dispatched to the leanest capable model — Llama 3.2 3B for simple tasks, 8B for mid-tier, and 70B only when truly needed. Unnecessary power consumption is blocked at the gate.
